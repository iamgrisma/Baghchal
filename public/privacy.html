Privacy Policy for Baghchal

Last Updated & Effective: October 7, 2026 | App: Baghchal (np.com.grisma.baghchal)

Summary: Baghchal does NOT collect, sell, or monetize your personal information. All game stats are kept locally on your device. Multiplayer games use secure peer-to-peer WebRTC connections with zero personal data tracking.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Baghchal: Tigers & Goats ("we", "our", or "the Game"), developed by Grisma (iamgrisma). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, usage, and protection when you use our Android application or web application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your digital privacy. The application does not collect any of the following:

  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect names, phone numbers, home addresses, or emails.
  • No Hardware Identifiers: We do not track IMEI, MAC addresses, or Advertising IDs (GAID).
  • No Device Sensors or Media: We do not request access to Camera, Microphone, Photo Library, Contacts, or Location services.
  • No Commercial Tracking: We do not use third-party behavioral analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Local Device Data Storage

The Game saves data strictly on your local device (using Android LocalStorage / App Preferences) to provide basic functionality:

  • Game settings (audio volume, theme preference, timer options).
  • Offline player statistics (match history, wins, losses, win streak, difficulty progress).
  • Local custom player display name (only stored locally unless shared during a live peer-to-peer match).

You can erase this data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android device settings.

4. Peer-to-Peer Online Multiplayer (WebRTC)

When you play online multiplayer mode:

  • Matches operate using direct peer-to-peer WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication).
  • To establish a direct peer connection, standard networking protocols exchange temporary connection candidates (ICE/STUN/TURN). This network exchange occurs strictly to connect the two players' devices directly.
  • Game moves are validated via an immutable cryptographic ledger (SHA-256 blocks) exchanged directly between devices to prevent game tampering. No game records are stored on central servers.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure

The application relies on trusted infrastructure providers solely for technical connectivity:

  • Cloudflare: Provides secure content delivery and lightweight signaling coordination.
  • Google Play Services: Required for application distribution, secure downloads, and app updates.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Baghchal is a traditional heritage strategy game suitable for audiences of all ages, including children and families. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Permissions Justification

The Android application requests only minimal, necessary system permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Needed to play online peer-to-peer matches.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to detect online/offline connectivity status gracefully.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Needed to provide haptic feedback when pieces move or are captured.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
